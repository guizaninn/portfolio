About me

I'm Guilherme Zanin.

As a Senior Product Designer with over nine years of design experience, my journey has been enriched by a diverse background in Digital Games and Interaction Design. Having had the opportunity to work across industries such as Investments, Banking, Digital Games, E-commerce, and Blockchain, I've cultivated a range of skills that include leadership, attention to detail, and an eye for visual appeal.

Throughout my career, I've found joy in leading teams and projects, striving to create experiences that resonate with users. One recent highlight includes my role as the leader of Card Squad at Bradesco, where I had the chance to collaborate with talented individuals and drive innovation within the team. It's through these experiences that I continue to learn and grow, always seeking to refine my craft and make meaningful contributions to the world of design.
