About Guilherme Zanin

This is the story of Guilherme, a product designer who never really stopped playing.

Scroll to turn the page

🎮

It started with 🕹️digital games. Then 👆interaction design: how people and screens talk to each other. He wanted to make things people enjoy using.

👾

In 2018 he joined 🐉Hoplon, a game studio, as a UI & graphic designer. Three years of designing for players, who never forgive a confusing screen.

🛒

In 2021 the players became shoppers. At 🛍️Checkcommerce he designed for e-commerce, where every extra click can cost a sale.

🧭

Then came ✏️VASTUU Design, a studio with clients in investments, blockchain and beyond. Every new client meant a new industry to learn.

In 2022 he joined 🏦Bradesco, the second largest private bank in Brazil, designing for more than 70 million people.

There he came to lead the 💳Card Squad, and found out that helping a team do its best work feels as good as doing his own.

📚

He never stopped studying: design leadership, data design, design systems, UX research, strategic design. Nine years in, he’s still a student.

Off the screen, you’ll find him ⚽playing football, 🎣fishing, or on a road trip with a 🧉mate in hand.

Or somewhere quiet, making ☕coffee with a view.

Hi! I’m Guilherme. I like building useful things with good people.
